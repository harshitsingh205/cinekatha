Android 7.0+ • Kotlin • Jetpack Compose

Download anything. From anywhere. In any format.

UniversalDL is an Android media downloader for videos, audio, GIFs, and photos across YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, X (Twitter), TikTok, Twitch, Reddit, and 1,000+ sites—complete with a built-in media player and a YouTube/Twitch clip cutter.

How to Use

  1. Copy a link from any supported app or site.
  2. Paste it into UniversalDL or share directly to the app.
  3. Pick format & quality: video, audio, GIF, or photo.
  4. Optionally trim exact start/end times in Clip Cutter.
  5. Download and play immediately in the built-in player.

Core Capabilities

Designed for multi-format extraction, precise trimming, and smooth offline playback.

Any Format, Any Resolution

Save videos (MP4, WebM, MKV from 144p to 4K), audio (MP3, M4A, OPUS, WAV), animated GIFs, and full-quality photos or carousels.

YouTube & Twitch Clip Cutter

Select exact start and end timestamps to download only the clip you need without saving an entire video file.

Built-In Player & Queue

Play audio in the background or watch with video gesture controls while managing multi-threaded pause, resume, and queue tasks.

Install & Build from Source

Requires Android Studio (latest stable), JDK 17, and Android SDK 34.

Install via APK

Download the latest UniversalDL.apk from the project Releases page, allow installing from unknown sources if prompted, paste a link, and start downloading.

Build with Gradle

Clone the repository and compile a debug build locally:

git clone https://github.com/SweetSamosa/UniversalDL.git
cd UniversalDL
./gradlew assembleDebug
./gradlew installDebug

Privacy Policy

UniversalDL Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Information Handling

UniversalDL is built for local media downloading and playback on your device. Link inputs, format choices, and download queues are processed to fulfill your requested downloads.

2. Network Requests & Third-Party Sites

When you paste or share a URL, the app connects to the target source or media extractor required to fetch stream metadata and media files. External websites operate under their own privacy policies and terms of service.

3. Local Storage & Preferences

App settings (such as theme mode, download preferences, and local privacy choices) are stored locally rather than sold or brokered to third parties.

Local Diagnostics & Crash Preference
Stored locally in your browser via localStorage.

4. Responsibility & Copyright

Use UniversalDL strictly for personal use and only for content you own or have explicit permission to download. Respect applicable copyright laws and platform terms of service.
